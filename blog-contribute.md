<section class="page-hero page-hero-learn"><p class="eyebrow">WRITE · SHARE · EVOLVE</p><h1>Have an idea to share?<br><span>Let's shape it together.</span></h1><p>CLP articles are contributed in Markdown and published only after editorial approval.</p></section>
<section class="section editorial-article-body"><h2>What a good CLP article includes</h2><ul><li>A clear title and a reader-focused summary.</li><li>A useful explanation with descriptive headings and examples.</li><li>A practical action, reflection or checklist.</li><li>Reliable sources, accurate attribution and image usage rights.</li><li>Accessible images, descriptive alternative text and working links.</li></ul><h2>Submit by email</h2><p>CLP will accept email submissions once a monitored contribution inbox is confirmed. The address is not configured yet, so please do not guess an email address. Prepare your email with the subject <strong>CLP Blog Contribution — [Article title]</strong> and include your name, article title, 1–2 sentence summary, category, full article text or attached Markdown file, source links, image credits and permission details.</p><p>Until the contribution inbox is published, use the repository submission route below or keep your draft ready. Sending a submission does not guarantee publication.</p><h2>How approval works</h2><ol><li>Prepare your article using the <a href="https://github.com/sanjeevkshu/clp/blob/master/docs/templates/blog-post-template.md" rel="noopener noreferrer">CLP blog article template</a>.</li><li>Submit the Markdown file in a pull request to the CLP repository.</li><li>The editor reviews accuracy, relevance, tone, attribution, accessibility and metadata.</li><li>Respond to requested changes in the same pull request.</li><li>Publication happens only when an approved pull request is merged into the production branch.</li></ol><p>Submitting an article does not guarantee publication. Please do not include confidential, personal or copyrighted material without permission.</p><a class="learn-card-button" href="https://github.com/sanjeevkshu/clp" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open the CLP repository ↗</a></section>

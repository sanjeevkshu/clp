# CLP YouTube channel plan

## Positioning
CLP — Continuous Learning & Progressive Evolution. **Learn. Apply. Evolve.** Brand-led, conversational Hinglish content for curious minds and professionals navigating change.

## Four recurring series
- **CLP Decodes:** complex ideas made simple.
- **CLP Masterclass:** practical skills and frameworks.
- **CLP Perspectives:** experts and diverse viewpoints.
- **CLP Evolution Lab:** experiments and learning challenges.

## First six main videos
1. AI Agents: Chatbot Se Aage Kya?
2. AI-Ready Kaise Banein? A 30-Day Learning Plan
3. AI, Data aur Digital Transformation: Inka Connection Kya Hai?
4. Busy Schedule Mein Learning Habit Kaise Banayein?
5. Future Mein Kaunsi Skills Valuable Hongi?
6. 7-Day AI Productivity Challenge

Alternate a main video and a derived Short each week. Track audience retention, returning viewers, meaningful comments and challenge participation monthly.

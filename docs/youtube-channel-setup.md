# CLP YouTube channel setup kit

## Brand
- **Channel name (recommended):** CLP — Learn. Apply. Evolve.
- **Brand expansion:** Continuous Learning & Progressive Evolution
- **Handle to try:** @CLPLearnApplyEvolve (availability must be checked in YouTube Studio)
- **Tone:** Clear, warm, curious, credible; conversational Hinglish
- **Visual system:** Deep navy, aquatic blue, violet; use the approved circular CLP logo as source of truth

## Channel setup sequence
1. Sign in to YouTube with the Google account intended to own CLP.
2. Create a channel with the recommended name; check handle availability and choose the closest clean match if needed.
3. In YouTube Studio → Customization → Profile, add the approved circular logo, banner, description, and website link.
4. In Customization → Home, enable a channel trailer for people who haven't subscribed; later feature the first full episode for returning subscribers.
5. Create the playlist **Start Here | CLP** for the trailer and channel introduction. Create **CLP Decodes** for explainers.
6. Verify account/feature eligibility and complete any phone or identity verification YouTube requests.
7. Upload the launch trailer as Unlisted first. Check sound, captions, crop, thumbnail, links, and playback on mobile.
8. Publish when the review is complete. Add the actual channel and trailer URLs to the website only after they exist and are verified.

## Banner guidance
Use YouTube's current channel-art template when exporting. Design a 2560×1440 px canvas, keeping the logo, channel name and tagline inside the central safe area so they remain visible on mobile, desktop and TV crops. Don't stretch the logo or add a second tagline lockup if the approved logo already contains one.

## First-month content sequence
1. Launch trailer: Meet CLP | Learn. Apply. Evolve.
2. CLP Decodes: AI Agents: Chatbot Se Aage Kya?
3. Short derived from the AI agents episode: chatbot vs agent in one practical example.
4. AI-Ready Kaise Banein? A 30-Day Learning Plan.

Keep a sustainable cadence: alternate a 4–6 minute main video with a useful Short or challenge. With a 2–4 hour weekly budget, prioritise clarity and consistency over daily posting.

## Account and publishing safeguards
- Keep ownership and recovery details with the channel owner; never put passwords or recovery codes in project files.
- Use only approved logo files and licensed/original footage, fonts and music.
- Review automated captions and any AI-generated claims before publishing.
- For the first trailer, avoid a hard subscribe pitch; invite viewers to explore and learn with CLP.
- Don't publish until the channel and video URLs are real and verified.

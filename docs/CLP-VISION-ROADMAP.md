# CLP Vision, Roadmap and Milestones

**Status:** Living, owner-governed 12-week execution plan  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-05  
**Brand promise:** Learn. Apply. Evolve.

## Vision
CLP = Continuous Learning and Progressive Evolution. CLP helps people make sense of change, build practical capability, apply what they learn, and evolve through continuous learning. The website is the canonical brand and learning destination; engagement channels extend discovery, conversation, and participation. Preserve approved wording and update only through an explicit owner decision.

## Roadmap integrity and phase mapping
A phase is defined by its outcome, not by the latest PR or one surface shipped. Every feature must trace to a phase objective and milestone. Implementation/deployment evidence for one deliverable does not, by itself, establish phase completion.

### Phase 1 — Establish CLP (Week 1 callout)
**Objective:** Establish the brand and its first engagement surface.

| Milestone | Outcome | Status |
|---|---|---|
| P1-M1 Website / brand launch | Public website is the canonical brand and learning destination. | Complete — owner-confirmed; Week 1 |
| P1-M2 YouTube / channel launch | Establish channel-based engagement and learning content surface. | Complete — owner-confirmed; Week 1 |

### Phase 2 — Extend social reach and engagement
**Objective:** Set up social core extensions to elevate reach and create additional engagement surfaces. This phase-level grouping is retained alongside the week-by-week execution plan; do not assume every Phase 2 milestone is complete merely because Week 2 is current.

| Milestone | Outcome | Status |
|---|---|---|
| P2-M1 Social connection surface | Connect experience links to verified official social destinations. | Implemented/merged; retain deployment evidence |
| P2-M2 Channel governance | Define account ownership, brand consistency, publishing roles, moderation, and link verification. | Planned / validate in current execution |
| P2-M3 Content distribution | Establish repeatable cross-surface publishing connected to relevant CLP learning resources. | Planned |
| P2-M4 Reach and engagement measurement | Define privacy-aware measures and review cadence for discovery, engagement, and onward learning. | Planned; aligns with Weeks 9–12 review |

Do not call a platform official until its destination and ownership are verified. Link presence alone is not evidence of audience growth. Set numeric targets only after owner-approved baselines. Profile and launch-copy preparation is captured in [Social Launch Pack](social-launch-pack.md); account activation and actual publication remain owner actions until evidenced.

## Status definitions
- Planned — agreed intent, not started.
- In progress — active design/build or current execution.
- Implemented — source change exists.
- Build verified — relevant automated checks passed.
- Preview deployed — exact revision deployed to preview.
- Visually/behaviorally verified — rendered behavior checked.
- Production verified — exact revision is live and checked.
- Complete — acceptance and outcome evidence met; owner confirms.

## Feature-to-roadmap traceability
Each feature record must identify week and phase/milestone (or cross-phase rationale), audience and outcome, scope/non-goals, dependencies, functional acceptance criteria, architecture/integration/data contracts, design and accessibility contracts, NFR/QE evidence, AI/agentic portability and human approval boundaries, documentation changes, and exact PR/SHA/preview/production evidence. Use [Feature Spec Template](templates/feature-spec-template.md).

## Status updates
Update this roadmap or its linked status record in the same feature PR whenever milestone state, acceptance evidence, scope, or dependencies change. Chat history is context, not the durable status source.

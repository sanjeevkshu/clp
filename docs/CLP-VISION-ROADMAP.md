# CLP Vision, Roadmap and Milestones

**Status:** Living plan, owner-governed  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-05  
**Brand promise:** Learn. Apply. Evolve.

## Vision
CLP helps people make sense of change, build practical capability, apply what they learn, and evolve through continuous learning. The website is the canonical brand and learning destination; engagement channels extend discovery, conversation, and participation. This is a working articulation; preserve approved wording and update only through an explicit owner decision.

## Roadmap integrity
A phase is defined by its outcome, not by the latest PR or one surface shipped. Every feature must trace to a phase objective and milestone. Implementation/deployment evidence for one deliverable does not, by itself, establish phase completion.

Use status labels consistently:
- Planned — agreed intent, not started.
- In progress — active design/build.
- Implemented — source change exists.
- Build verified — relevant automated checks passed.
- Preview deployed — exact revision deployed to preview.
- Visually/behaviorally verified — rendered behavior checked.
- Production verified — exact revision is live and checked.
- Complete — acceptance and outcome evidence met; owner confirms.

## Phase 1 — Establish CLP
**Objective:** Establish the brand and its first engagement surface.

| Milestone | Outcome | Status |
|---|---|---|
| P1-M1 Website / brand launch | Public website is the canonical brand and learning destination. | Complete — owner-confirmed |
| P1-M2 YouTube / channel launch | Establish channel-based engagement and learning content surface. | Complete — owner-confirmed |

## Phase 2 — Extend social reach and engagement
**Objective:** Set up social core extensions to elevate reach and create additional engagement surfaces.

| Milestone | Outcome | Status |
|---|---|---|
| P2-M1 Social connection surface | Connect experience links to verified official social destinations. | Implemented/merged; maintain deployment evidence |
| P2-M2 Channel governance | Define account ownership, brand consistency, publishing roles, moderation, and link verification. | To plan |
| P2-M3 Content distribution | Establish repeatable cross-surface publishing connected to relevant CLP learning resources. | To plan |
| P2-M4 Reach and engagement measurement | Define privacy-aware measures and review cadence for discovery, engagement, and onward learning. | To plan |

Do not call a platform official until its destination and ownership are verified. Link presence alone is not evidence of audience growth. Set numeric targets only after owner-approved baselines.

## Phase 2 acceptance direction
- Social destinations are accurate, owned, and governed.
- Content is distributed consistently from CLP's canonical surfaces.
- Posts provide a meaningful path to learning or engagement.
- Measurement is privacy-aware and tied to decisions.
- Evidence supports owner review before expanding channels or advancing phases.

## Feature-to-roadmap traceability
Each feature record must identify phase/milestone (or cross-phase rationale), audience and outcome, scope/non-goals, dependencies, functional acceptance criteria, architecture/integration/data contracts, design and accessibility contracts, NFR/QE evidence, AI/agentic portability and human approval boundaries, documentation changes, and exact PR/SHA/preview/production evidence.

## Status updates
Update this roadmap or its linked status record in the same feature PR whenever milestone state, acceptance evidence, scope, or dependencies change. Chat history is context, not the durable status source.

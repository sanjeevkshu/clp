<section class="page-hero page-hero-about" style="--page-hero-image:url('{{ '/assets/images/card-evolve.jpg' | relative_url }}')"><p class="eyebrow">OUR BELIEF</p><h1>The world evolves.<br><span>So should we.</span></h1><p>CLP stands for Continuous Learning & Progressive Evolution — a learning brand built around curiosity, practical application and collective progress.</p></section><section class="section"><div class="prose"><h2>Why CLP exists</h2><p>Change is constant. New technologies emerge, professional expectations shift, and the problems we face become more interconnected. CLP exists to make learning approachable and useful, so people can turn new ideas into better decisions and meaningful progress.</p><h2>Our learning loop</h2><div class="loop-grid"><article><strong>01 · Learn</strong><p>Understand a concept, skill or new perspective.</p></article><article><strong>02 · Apply</strong><p>Try it on a real problem and observe the outcome.</p></article><article><strong>03 · Evolve</strong><p>Reflect, improve and share what you discovered.</p></article></div><h2>What we value</h2><ul><li>Curiosity over assumptions.</li><li>Practical learning over empty hype.</li><li>Evidence, context and responsible use of technology.</li><li>Respectful disagreement and diverse perspectives.</li><li>Learning with people, not only from content.</li></ul><p>CLP begins as a content-led learning brand and aims to grow into a community where learners contribute questions, experiences and projects.</p></div></section>

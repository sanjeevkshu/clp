    <p class="episode-intro">A production-ready, conversational Hinglish episode that explains what agent-style AI workflows can do, where they help, and why human judgement still matters.</p>

  <div class="episode-shell">
    <div class="episode-callout"><strong>Episode promise</strong><br>By the end, viewers should be able to distinguish a typical chatbot interaction from a multi-step agent-style workflow—and identify one safe task to experiment with.</div>
    <section class="episode-section" aria-labelledby="episode-script-title">
      <h2 id="episode-script-title">01 / Voice-over script</h2>
      <div class="script-beat"><div class="script-time">00:00–00:15</div><div><h3>The hook</h3><p>Duniya mein AI tools tezi se badh rahe hain. Aaj hum AI se sawaal poochte hain aur jawab lete hain. Lekin AI agent ka idea isse ek step aage jaata hai: goal samajhna, steps plan karna, tools use karna aur task ke beech progress check karna. Toh chatbot aur AI agent mein actual difference kya hai?</p></div></div>
      <div class="script-beat"><div class="script-time">00:15–00:40</div><div><h3>Meet CLP</h3><p>Welcome to CLP — Continuous Learning &amp; Progressive Evolution. Yahan hum naye ideas ko simple language mein samjhenge, practical examples explore karenge, aur knowledge ko action mein badlenge. CLP ka principle hai: Learn. Apply. Evolve.</p></div></div>
      <div class="script-beat"><div class="script-time">00:40–01:30</div><div><h3>Chatbot vs agent</h3><p>Ek typical chatbot conversation mein aap prompt dete hain aur model response deta hai. Agent-style workflow mein system ko ek goal diya ja sakta hai; woh task ko steps mein tod sakta hai, approved tools use kar sakta hai, results check kar sakta hai aur zaroorat padne par next step choose kar sakta hai. Har product ka behaviour alag hota hai — sirf agent label dekhkar assume mat kijiye ki system fully autonomous ya reliable hai.</p></div></div>
      <div class="script-beat"><div class="script-time">01:30–02:30</div><div><h3>A practical example</h3><p>Maan lijiye aapko kisi topic par weekly research brief banana hai. A simple workflow sources collect karega, key points organise karega, citations ke saath draft banayega, aur aapke review ke liye questions highlight karega. Aap source quality verify karenge, claims check karenge aur final brief approve karenge. Agent time bacha sakta hai, lekin judgement ki responsibility khatam nahi hoti.</p></div></div>
      <div class="script-beat"><div class="script-time">02:30–03:30</div><div><h3>Where to be careful</h3><p>Agents galat assumptions le sakte hain, weak sources choose kar sakte hain, tools ko unexpected tareeke se use kar sakte hain, ya incomplete task ko complete samajh sakte hain. Isliye clear permissions, limited access, checkpoints, reliable sources aur human approval important hain — especially jab external actions ya sensitive data involved ho.</p></div></div>
      <div class="script-beat"><div class="script-time">03:30–04:15</div><div><h3>Try it safely</h3><p>Ek chhota exercise try kijiye. Apne week ka ek repetitive, low-risk task choose karein. Uske steps likhein. Phir identify karein kaunse steps AI assist kar sakta hai, kaunse outputs verify karne honge, aur final approval kaun dega. Pehle small experiment karein; impact measure karein.</p></div></div>
      <div class="script-beat"><div class="script-time">04:15–04:40</div><div><h3>Close and invite participation</h3><p>Toh AI agent ka matlab magic nahi. It is a workflow that may plan and act across steps, within the tools and permissions it has. Aap apne daily work ka kaunsa task AI agent se assist karwana chahenge? Comment mein batayein. CLP ke saath seekhte rahiye, apply karte rahiye, aur evolve karte rahiye.</p></div></div>
    </section>

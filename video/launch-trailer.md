# CLP launch trailer — production brief

**Purpose:** Introduce CLP in 40–45 seconds. This is the brand trailer, not Episode 01.  
**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 24/25 fps; make a 9:16 cut after the master is approved.  
**Style:** Cinematic, modern, human, optimistic. Deep navy, aquatic blue and violet; clean typography, restrained motion, no greenish teal. Use the approved circular CLP logo when available.  
**Audio:** Warm, confident conversational Hinglish voice-over; subtle licensed ambient/electronic bed. Voice should feel thoughtful and credible, not like a loud advertisement.

## Voice-over script (approx. 42 seconds)

**0:00–0:05 — The world is changing**  
“Duniya tezi se badal rahi hai. Technology, work, aur opportunities — sab kuch.”

**0:05–0:12 — The human question**  
“Lekin sirf naye trends jaanna kaafi nahi. Zaroori hai samajhna, seekhna… aur use real life mein apply karna.”

**0:12–0:21 — Brand reveal**  
“Welcome to CLP — Continuous Learning & Progressive Evolution.”

**0:21–0:32 — What viewers get**  
“Yahan milenge complex ideas ke simple explainers, practical skills, naye perspectives, aur chhote experiments jo aapko aage badhne mein help karein.”

**0:32–0:39 — Brand promise**  
“Chahe AI ho, future of work, ya everyday learning — har step ka focus hai meaningful progress.”

**0:39–0:44 — Close**  
“CLP. Learn. Apply. Evolve. Chaliye, shuru karte hain.”

## Edit and accessibility notes
- Burn in accurate captions; keep each caption to one or two short lines.
- Avoid tiny text, rapid flashing, overpacked transitions, and fake interface screenshots.
- Use original or properly licensed footage, sound effects, and music; retain license/source notes.
- Keep the logo and essential text within the central safe area so the video can be cropped for mobile.
- Mix voice-over clearly above music. End with a short clean logo hold.
- Export master as MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio); review on phone speakers and headphones before upload.
